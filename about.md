I'm German Gerken. I write about measuring the behaviour of LLM agents in production and
about the engineering behind the tools that do it.

I maintain [JourneyGraph](https://github.com/GermanGerken/journeygraph), a local-first,
open-source command-line tool that analyses agent trace exports and shows what changed
between two versions.

Every number in these articles comes from a measurement that can be reproduced. When data
is synthetic, the article says so.

Find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/GermanGerken).
